Privacy Policy

Iris · Effective 2026-10-20

Iris is an internal social-media scheduling and community-management tool operated by its owner (the "Operator"). It is used only to manage Threads and Instagram accounts the Operator owns. Iris is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

What Iris accesses

Through the Threads API and the Instagram API, with the account owner's authorization, Iris accesses: the connected account's profile (ID, username), the account's own posts and replies, replies and reactions to those posts, post insights (views, likes, replies, reposts), and public posts returned by keyword search. Iris publishes posts and replies that an operator has written and approved.

What Iris stores

Iris stores API access tokens for the connected accounts, the scheduling queue, a log of published post and reply IDs, and keyword-search candidates (post ID, username, text excerpt, timestamp) for up to 30 days. Data is stored on machines controlled by the Operator and is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. Content from other users' public posts is used only to decide whether to reply and is never republished.

How long

Access tokens are kept while an account stays connected and are deleted when the account is disconnected. Search candidates are deleted after 30 days. Publishing logs are kept for operations and analytics.

Your choices

Any connected account can be disconnected at any time from the Threads or Instagram app (Settings → Account → Website permissions / Apps and websites), which invalidates Iris's token. To request deletion of any stored data, see the Data Deletion page.
